Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how Africana AI handles information when you visit our website or use its features, including accounts, community posts, job listings, and marketplace services. Different features may request different information. Where a feature provides a separate notice, read that notice together with this policy.

Information you provide

Depending on the feature, you may provide account and profile details, contact information, job or business listings, messages, comments, images, and information needed to respond to a support request. Please avoid publishing sensitive personal information about yourself or another person in a public post or listing.

Information collected through use

When you use the site, our systems and service providers may process technical information such as your browser, device, approximate connection details, pages requested, and security or error logs. Cookies or similar technologies may be used to support essential site functions, remember preferences, protect accounts, or measure service performance, depending on your settings and the feature in use.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate and secure the site, provide requested features, display content you choose to publish, communicate about your account or support request, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve our services. If you use a feature involving automated or AI-assisted tools, information you submit may be processed to provide that feature; review its on-screen guidance before sharing confidential material.

Public content and service providers

Posts, listings, and profile details you choose to make public may be visible to other users and accessible through public web pages. We may use service providers to host, secure, or operate parts of the service, and may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect people, the service, or legal rights. Providers are expected to handle information for their service role under their applicable terms.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described above, to maintain accounts and records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. We use safeguards intended to protect information, but no website or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and questions

You can review or update some account details through the site. To ask a privacy question or request help with your information, contact info@africanaai.info. We may need to verify your request before taking action. This policy may be updated as the service changes; the effective date above indicates when this version applies.

This page provides general information about the service's privacy practices and is not legal advice.
